Gemba Kaizen: Standardized Work Optimization & Waste Elimination

Presenter: ARUNA D R (#50031)
Area: GD HEAD Machining Line
Reviewer: BINDUKUMAR S (GL) / GOVINDA R (AM)
1. Worksite Problem (Komarigoto) & 21 KVP Step 3-4
Station / Process:
GD HEAD Machining Line • OP-120 Clamping Station
Gemba Struggle Observed:
Operator leaves station envelope and walks 14m to fetch hex wrench on every engine head loading, causing operator fatigue and cycle time bottleneck.
Selected 21 Kaizen Viewpoints (Observation Checklist):
2. Root Cause Analysis (5-Why) Step 5-6
Why 1: Operator cycle time exceeds takt by 14 seconds? → Operator leaves station envelope to find clamping tool.
Why 2: Why leave station envelope? → Tool stored on general shared rack 7 meters away.
Why 3: Why stored away? → No dedicated station-mounted tool presentation holster.
Why 4: Why manual hex wrench used? → Standard work relied on 6-turn threaded bolt fixture.
Root Cause: Absence of point-of-use Standardized Work tool placement and slow mechanical fastening.
3. Implemented Countermeasure (Kaizen) Step 7-8
Fabricated point-of-use magnetic tool holster directly on fixture + installed quick cam-toggle clamp replacing manual 6-turn hex bolt.
Before Kaizen
• Walking distance: 14m/cycle
• Tool search delay: 28 sec
• Operator posture: bending/strain
After Kaizen
• Walking distance: 0m (100% eliminated)
• Clamping time: 4 sec (-24s)
• Operator posture: neutral & safe
4. Tangible Results & Horizontal Deployment Step 9-11
Cycle Time Reduction 64s → 40s (-24s / 37.5%)
Walking Distance Saved 14m → 0m (100% saved)
Annual Cost Savings &inr; 1,25,000 / annum
Ergonomic Safety REBA Score: 8 → 2 (Safe)
